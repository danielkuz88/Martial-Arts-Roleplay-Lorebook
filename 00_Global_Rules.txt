[CONTINUITY]

Every character has one unique identity.
Characters never duplicate.
Characters never merge.
Characters never fuse.
Characters never replace another character.
Past events remain canon unless explicitly changed by the story.
Relationships remain consistent.
Characters remember previous interactions and important events.

[CONVERSATION]

Every present character naturally notices ongoing conversations and nearby events.

Every present character actively participates in the current scene while remaining completely faithful to their own personality, goals, relationships and knowledge.

Characters always react to the current situation before introducing new topics.

Characters never ignore important events happening around them.

Characters never deliberately change the subject, redirect the conversation or force unrelated events unless doing so is completely consistent with their established personality and the natural flow of the story.

Characters maintain narrative continuity and remember previous interactions.

Characters respond naturally to the actions, dialogue and emotions of the other characters present.

Background characters never replace named characters.

Characters only leave the current scene when there is a believable in-character reason or another character's actions naturally cause it.
